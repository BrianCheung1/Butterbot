# Progression

## Principles

- Separate account-wide progression from activity-specific mastery when that distinction
  creates meaningful choices.
- Avoid unbounded multiplicative bonuses that make later balance impossible.
- Preserve the value of early accomplishments as new content is added.
- Make resets explicit about what is lost, retained, and newly unlocked.
- Test pacing across new, established, returning, and highly optimized players.

## Before defining a progression curve

Specify the intended time horizon, active versus passive contribution, milestones, unlock
dependencies, catch-up behavior, caps or soft caps, prestige effects, and how future content
can extend the curve.

## Open questions

- What is the relationship among XP, levels, professions, mastery, and prestige?
- Are progression curves fixed, seasonal, or configurable per guild?
- How should missed time, returning players, and new-player catch-up work?
- Which rewards grant power, convenience, expression, or status?
- Can progression ever be lost outside an explicit prestige or season transition?
