# Game design

## Goals

- Support meaningful play over months or years without requiring constant attention.
- Make short sessions useful while preserving longer-term goals.
- Keep rewards legible: players should understand what happened and why.
- Let systems combine without making one activity mandatory for all players.
- Preserve player trust through predictable rules and recoverable operations.

## Planned system space

The product may eventually include currency and banking, transfers, inventories, items,
equipment, gathering activities, professions, levels, shops, crafting, quests, achievements,
collections, prestige, leaderboards, trading, events, sinks, and seasons. This is a roadmap
space, not an approved feature list or implementation order.

## Design requirements for future features

Each feature proposal should identify its player loop, inputs, outputs, cadence, failure
states, interactions with existing systems, abuse cases, telemetry needs, and effect on old
content. Numerical tuning belongs in data or configuration once a concrete system exists.

## Open questions

- Is the game scoped per Discord guild, globally, or as a hybrid?
- What is the intended interaction cadence and target audience?
- Which activity forms the first playable core loop?
- How much competition, cooperation, and direct player conflict is desirable?
- What seasonal content persists, rotates, or resets?
